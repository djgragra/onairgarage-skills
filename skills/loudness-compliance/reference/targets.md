# Loudness targets (status: secondary sources, 2026-10-29)

Delivery specifications (a programme must meet them):

| Spec | Integrated | Tolerance | True peak max | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EBU R 128 | -23 LUFS | +-0.5 LU | -1 dBTP | Recommendation, not binding; broadcasters add their own conditions (for example a lower true-peak limit for data-reduced formats). R 128 also allows a wider tolerance for live programmes: check the current text |
| ATSC A/85 (US TV) | -24 LKFS | +-2 dB | -2 dBTP (recommended) | Mandatory for adverts under the US CALM Act |

Not included because not verified: Japan (ARIB TR-B32), UK (OFCOM/DPP), Australia (OP-59) and other national specs. Ask the user for the broadcaster's document.

Playback normalization of platforms (company policies, from a secondary table; they change):

| Platform | Reference loudness | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Spotify | about -14 LUFS | User setting can change it |
| YouTube | about -14 LUFS | Reported to reduce loud content, not to raise quiet content |
| Apple Music | about -16 LUFS | Applies when Sound Check is enabled |

Always verify with the platform's current documentation.

Measurement documents: EBU Tech 3341 (meter), Tech 3342 (LRA), ITU-R BS.1770 (algorithm).
